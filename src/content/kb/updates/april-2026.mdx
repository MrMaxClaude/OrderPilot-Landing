April was a big month. Highlights below.

## New: industry landing pages

We shipped dedicated landings for five industry / stack combinations:

- SAP customers on mid-market tier
- AFAS Profit users
- Manufacturing distributors
- Wholesale / distribution
- General mid-market

Each page walks through how OrderPilot applies to that specific operating model with sector-specific examples.

## Improved: AFAS Profit connector

The AFAS connector is now certified against the latest App Connector API. Notable changes:

- Nightly vendor master sync (was weekly)
- Automatic KostenplaatsCode derivation from requester email
- Re-post action available on all failed UpdateConnector calls

Existing AFAS customers don't need to do anything - the update deploys automatically.

## New: Knowledge Base

The page you're reading now. Structured into five sections:

1. Getting Started - onboarding guides
2. Integraties - ERP connectors, per-ERP
3. Tips & Best Practices - playbooks from production teams
4. FAQ - direct answers to the questions we hear most
5. Updates - this section, monthly

Search across all articles is client-side and indexes every article. Feedback at the bottom of each page goes directly to our product team.

## Improvements

- Validation queue now shows template-drift warnings when a supplier changes their layout
- Accuracy report gains a "By field" segmentation view
- PDF generation for ROI reports now runs under 8 seconds (was 12)

## Coming in May

We're working on:

- Microsoft Dynamics 365 BC connector (beta)
- Native SSO for Google Workspace and Entra ID
- An opt-in data residency option for EU customers

As always - if there's something you want us to prioritise, tell your account manager or drop it in the feedback widget on any article.
